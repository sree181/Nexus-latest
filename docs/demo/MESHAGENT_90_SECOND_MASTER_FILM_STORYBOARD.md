# meshAgent 90-Second Master Product Film

**Working title:** From Code Change to Governed Decision
**Audience:** CISO office, security leadership, engineering leadership, and enterprise buyers
**Running time:** 90 seconds
**Format:** 16:9 master, 1920 × 1080, 30 frames per second
**Author:** Manus AI

## Creative direction

The film should feel like an enterprise product demonstration, not a software advertisement. It begins inside the developer's real workflow, follows one security issue through Analyst operations, and ends with the CISO's governed decision and verified remediation. Product footage is the primary visual evidence. Motion graphics clarify relationships but never invent screens, data, or capabilities.

The visual system should match the light meshAgent presentation: warm white surfaces, dark navy typography, teal for verified evidence, amber for attention, and red only for blocked or overdue states. Camera moves should be restrained. Use 180–250 millisecond interface transitions, slow 6–10 percent crops into important evidence, and direct cuts between roles. Avoid generic artificial-intelligence imagery, typing montages, glowing brains, robots, and unsupported compliance language.

## Voice direction

Use one confident narrator with a measured, conversational delivery, with brief pauses after “Security,” “evidence,” and “independent decision.” The voice should sound informed rather than theatrical. Pronounce **meshAgent** as “Mesh Agent,” **hypermesh** as “hyper mesh,” **hypermeshai.com** as “hyper mesh A I dot com,” and **CISO** as “see-so” if the role title is added in an alternate edit.

Do not emphasize technical terms more than the business outcome. The words “digest,” “native,” and “separation of duties” should be delivered clearly but without slowing the film into an architecture lecture.

## Screen-capture plan

Capture a single fresh, authorized workflow in the same disposable Git branch. Record Cursor at 2560 × 1440 or higher with the editor zoom set so the prompt, branch, and package command remain readable after cropping. Record meshAgent at 1440p with browser zoom at 100 percent. Use separate clean takes for Developer Attention, Analyst Operations, review evidence, Decision Desk, exception detail, and Remediation Portfolio.

Before capture, remove unrelated notifications and tabs. Use synthetic names only for the demonstration identities, but create the workflow live rather than presenting seeded records as current client activity. Hide tokens, private repository URLs, terminal history, environment files, and any client information that has not been approved for recording.

## Editorial assembly

Use direct cuts when the role changes. Use a short match transition when one evidence node moves from Developer to Analyst or from Analyst to CISO. Keep the pointer visible only while it establishes cause and effect. Do not animate every interface control.

Place captions in the lower safe area, use no more than two lines, and keep them separate from product labels. The narration should remain intelligible without music. The music bed should be restrained electronic ambience at approximately −24 to −20 LUFS beneath speech. Deliver the master around −14 LUFS integrated, with true peak no higher than −1 dBTP.

## Required capture checklist

The final edit must visibly include a real Cursor prompt, Git repository context, an exactly pinned package check, the Developer review request, Analyst ownership and due time, a source-linked case, the CISO decision context, a native hypermesh evidence record, and an evidence-backed remediation outcome. If any live capture is unavailable, replace only that shot with a clearly current validated product recording. Do not fabricate the state in motion graphics.

## Deliverables derived from the master

The 90-second film is the source edit. The 30-second teaser and 15-second silent website loop reuse only verified product visuals and the approved closing frame. Each promotional version closes with **meshAgent**, **powered by hypermesh**, the product line, the repository-and-policy call to action, and **hypermeshai.com**. Neither derivative adds capabilities not shown in the master or supported by the release.
